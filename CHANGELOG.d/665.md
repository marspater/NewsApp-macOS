- Full notifications now run in three lanes with their own limits. Major news, covered by four or more publishers with a breaking headline, always notifies, and a burst of it becomes one grouped banner instead of being dropped. Starred feeds post at most one banner per feed every 20 minutes, grouping a busy feed's stories into "N new stories", and starring a feed never brings up its backlog. Other breaking and top-interest stories notify at most five times in any hour, counted across refreshes and relaunches. Stories that don't notify stay in the feed and the Briefing. The rules are in `docs/notifications.md`.
