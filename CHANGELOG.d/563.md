- Library backup: Back Up Library… (File menu or Settings › Storage) saves your stories, read history, saved stories, settings and World Tension history to one file, and Restore Library… brings such a file back and relaunches News, keeping the replaced library beside it as `news-before-restore-<date>-<time>.sqlite3` (newest two kept) so a restore can be undone; a damaged backup or one from a newer version is refused and your library stays as it was (#563).
