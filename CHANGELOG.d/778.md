- Scrolling no longer stutters while stories are classified or feeds refresh. Background classification publishes its results together once a second instead of re-rendering every list, card and the open reader for each story (150 classified stories: 150 publications become 1, and the list's main-thread time falls from about 3.2 s to under 0.1 s); a refresh publishes feed statuses once instead of once per feed; the reader re-reads publisher updates only when its own story changes; the list waits out a burst of changes before re-rendering, groups its page once per change, and cards share one date formatter, clean each summary once and cast their shadow from the card shape (#778).
- Lists load faster from a large library: pages walk a new date-order index instead of sorting every unread story (Today's first page on a 7,400-story library copy: about 190 ms to 4 ms; the 500-story snapshot each refresh publishes: about 125 ms to 23 ms), and a publisher's recurring images, which every story fetch checks, are remembered until new stories are stored or a story's images change, instead of being recomputed from every reader document (10 to 40 ms per fetch) (#778).
