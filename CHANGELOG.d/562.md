- First launch walks through a short welcome, one step at a time: what News does, choosing feeds by topic, sidebar sections, on-device story analysis, when News will ask about notifications, and a summary of your choices. Each step has its own illustration and a tip about a feature that is easy to miss. Back, Continue, Return and Escape move between steps. The steps slide in the direction of travel, and nothing moves when Reduce Motion is on. It appears once, and people upgrading from an earlier version never see it.
- News now asks for notification permission once a refresh has brought in stories and the welcome has closed, instead of at launch. With notifications off, it asks when you turn them on.
