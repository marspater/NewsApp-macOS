- Story matching computes its sentence embeddings on the Neural Engine: the flexible-length encoder ran entirely on the CPU, so the bundled MiniLM now holds one fixed-length function per text length (16, 32, 64 and 128 tokens, one shared copy of the weights) and loads with CPU and Neural Engine only, leaving the GPU to the interface. The vectors are unchanged (cosine at least 0.99996 against the previous model on the authored corpus), so stored embeddings stay valid; `./test.sh --compute-plan` reports where each model runs and how fast under every compute unit (#554).
