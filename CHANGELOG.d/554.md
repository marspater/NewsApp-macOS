- Story matching computes its sentence embeddings on the Neural Engine: the flexible-length encoder ran entirely on the CPU, so the bundled MiniLM now holds one fixed-length function per text length (16, 32, 64 and 128 tokens, one shared copy of the weights) and loads with CPU and Neural Engine only, leaving the GPU to the interface. The vectors are unchanged (cosine at least 0.99996 against the previous model on the authored corpus), so stored embeddings stay valid; `./test.sh --compute-plan` reports where each model runs and how fast under every compute unit; embeddings now run in batches of at most 64 texts, since one batch of a few thousand exhausted the system's IOSurface memory and ended the process (#554).
- Developer tooling: `compress_models.py` exports both models as fixed-length functions and measures 8-bit weight compression; int8 and 8-bit palettization halve the bundle and leave the replays unchanged, but miss the parity bar, so both models stay fp16 (#554).
- The pair model also runs on the Neural Engine at the length a pair needs (64, 128 or 256 tokens) instead of always 256: one model prediction takes about 0.7 ms instead of 1.8 ms, with unchanged probabilities and decisions (#554).
