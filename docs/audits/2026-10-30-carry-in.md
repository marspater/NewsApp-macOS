# Two-thirds carry-in measured (#738, 30 October 2026)

#627 traced three of the seven v12 wrong links on the 24 October holdout to the whole-event admission rule: a report joins an event when it matches one member and is compatible with two thirds of them (`EventMatchPolicy.compatibleShare = 2/3`). #738 asked for the carry-in to be measured on the fresh library holdout and for one tightening to be adopted only if strict precision rises with no loss on the authored sets.

## Setup

- Matcher v14 (#761), pair cutoff 0.90.
- Fresh library holdout: `~/news-eval/holdout-2026-10-29/library-holdout-2026-10-29.json` (SHA-256 `a0b681bc…3545`), clock pin `NEWS_HOLDOUT_NOW=1793300435`, re-clustered copy of the 29 October library.
- Authored sets: `--matcher-regressions` (24 / 0 at v14: #761 adds the storm family f17, so the corpus there has one more true pair than main's 23 / 0) and `--story-regressions` (22 event and 30 story pairs, nothing unexpected at v14).
- Candidates, each through an existing evaluation knob (`StoryCorpus.policyFromEnvironment`):
  - every member compatible: `NEWS_COMPATIBLE_SHARE=1`, a per-member check;
  - compatible only when matching: `NEWS_COMPATIBLE_REQUIRES_MATCH=1`.

## Results

| Policy | Strict event (same / related / different) | Strict precision | Story level (same + related / different) | Matcher regressions | Story regressions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v14 (two thirds) | 6 / 3 / 3 | 0.500 | 7 + 3 / 4 | 24 / 0 | unchanged |
| every member compatible | 6 / 1 / 0 | 0.857 | 7 + 1 / 4 | 24 / 0 | 6 pairs changed |
| compatible only when matching | 2 / 1 / 0 | 0.667 | 4 + 2 / 4 | 24 / 0 | changed |

- **Every member compatible** removes all three wrong strict links on the holdout without losing a same-event link, so the carry-in is real. On the authored story set it splits the Nigeria report "isolates first Ebola patient in Lagos" (s20-d) from the other three Ebola reports. That fixes two known event gaps (Kenya and Nigeria merged), but it loses a true event pair (the two Nigeria reports) and three true story pairs (the story of Ebola cases in two countries).
- **Compatible only when matching** loses four of the six same-event links.

## Decision

Neither candidate passes: the first loses true pairs on the authored story set, the second loses holdout recall. The two-thirds rule stays. Finer settings of the same knob add little for small events: the required count is `ceil(n × share)`, so for three members every share above two thirds already requires all three, and for four members a share up to three quarters requires three (as two thirds does) and any higher share requires all four. Only events of five or more members see a setting between the two measured ones.

The measurement points at the next lever: the per-member check is right for the holdout's wrong links, and what it breaks is a same-event pair whose members share only the place ("Nigeria", "Lagos") and the disease. A per-member check that exempts pairs sharing a specific anchor of the event's first report would keep s20-c and s20-d together; it is not built here.
