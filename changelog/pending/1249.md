- The event overview timeline now shows a date range ("from 19 to 28 November", "November 19–28") or a list of dates in one phrase ("on 3 and 5 November") as one row, written as the source states it, with no invented start, end or year. It sits at its earliest date, after any single date on that day, and a range that starts after the article was published is a Plan. A range alone, or with a date inside it, is no timeline, and a range or list is never a Briefing turning point or the start of a "day N" count. Sentences with unrelated second dates, reversed ranges or a range plus another date stay out. Overviews cached under analysis version 16 are regenerated (#1249).
