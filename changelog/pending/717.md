- Adds Compare Perspectives (#717): an event overview with two or more publishers' reports offers a "Compare Perspectives" action that opens a sheet with two reports side by side from 1,200 pt wide, or stacked when narrower. The sheet compares the reports by meaning on this Mac, marks each passage "Both report", "Reported differently" or "Only in this report", scrolls the other report to a matching passage and reads each column in order with VoiceOver. It never labels a publisher.
