- Adds entity dossiers (#716): a story's people, organizations and places, read on this Mac with Apple's NaturalLanguage after clustering, now open a popover from an "In this story" row in the reader and the event overview, with 30 days of coverage, top publishers, related entities and recent stories (schema version 36).
- Relationships between entities are stored as a typed, weighted adjacency list in SQLite, derived from the stories two entities share; a dossier shows each count. Global centrality is computed in background passes only (after an indexing pass and in the system's maintenance windows, never when a dossier opens), and a dossier ranks its related entities with a bounded Personalized PageRank walk. Low Power Mode and thermal pressure defer the index, and Clear AI Analysis removes it.
