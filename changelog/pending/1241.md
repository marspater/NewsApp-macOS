- The event overview timeline no longer labels a date stated in the source as a reporting date. Timeline items now say what their date is: a calendar date stated in the source (with its precision, day or month), a stated anchor such as a time of day, "yesterday morning" or a quarter, or a publication date. Only a publication date reads "Reported", and an item without a date shows none. A stated date keeps its precision without an invented year, plans keep their Plan badge and stated date, and a stored "Scheduled" placeholder is dropped when it is read. The stated-date builder is the only timeline assembler. The Briefing story thread orders its rows by a separate ordering-only date, shows their stated text and leaves anchors and publication dates out. It appears only with two distinct occurrence dates, so a day written twice or a month with a day inside it is one date. It shows "day N" only when its first row is a day-precision date.
