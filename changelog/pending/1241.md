- The event overview timeline no longer labels a date stated in the source as a reporting date. Timeline items now say what their date is: a calendar date stated in the source, a stated anchor such as a time of day, "yesterday morning" or a quarter, or a publication date. Only a publication date reads "Reported". A stated date keeps its precision without an invented year, plans keep their Plan badge and stated date, and a bare "Scheduled" is no longer shown as a date. The stated-date builder is the only timeline assembler. The Briefing story thread orders its items by a separate ordering-only date, shows their stated text and leaves relative anchors out.
