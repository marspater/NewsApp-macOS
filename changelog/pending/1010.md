- A notification photo or banner photo that fails to load now logs the stage that refused it (fetch, decode, crop, encode, size or attach) with the content type and byte count received, never the address. Palette PNG images are covered by a regression through the reader decode and the notification attachment.
- Image requests ask the host for PNG or JPEG instead of its WebP or AVIF variant, so a notification photo decodes the same bytes the reader sees (#1010).
