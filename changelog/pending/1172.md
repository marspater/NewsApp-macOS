- World Tension adds a "Near you" lens (#1172). Where the Mac's Region names a country a war is next door to (Poland, the Baltic states, Finland, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Moldova), the reading adds up to 15° for the past week's stories in your own library: 3 to 12° by how many publishers name Ukraine, Belarus or Russia with fighting or attacks, and 1 to 3° by how many name your country with a crisis, a protest, an attack or a security cue. The score, level, sidebar and Briefing strip show the total; the sheet shows the world reading beside "Near you +N°" and a Near You group with what was counted. It counts keywords in titles, not the model, and nothing leaves your Mac; readings older than 30 days and other regions are unchanged.
