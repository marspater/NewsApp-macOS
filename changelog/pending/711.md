- Reader: select a passage and press ⌘Y to highlight it, or ⇧⌘Y to add a private note. Highlights and notes stay on this Mac, find their words again when a publisher's text is extracted anew, show in the Annotations inspector (⌥⌘I) and in Library › Notes & Highlights, where they are searchable. A story with a highlight or note is not pruned by retention (#711).
